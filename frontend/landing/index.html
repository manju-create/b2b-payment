Detect. Diagnose. Recover.

An end-to-end automated workflow powered by intelligent negotiation stance physics.

STEP 01
🔍

DETECT

AI scans overdue invoices and scores each debtor by payment risk using real accounts receivable data.

STEP 02
🧠

DIAGNOSE

Root cause analysis identifies WHY the invoice is unpaid — cashflow, dispute, oversight, or avoidance.

STEP 03
💰

RECOVER

AI agent negotiates directly with debtor, collects proof if needed, builds a payment plan, and closes the transaction via Razorpay.

🛡️ Every action logged. Compliant escalation if needed. Full audit trail always.
